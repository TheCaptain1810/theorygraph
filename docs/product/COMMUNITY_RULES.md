# Community Rules and Content Model

## Purpose
Make nuanced film analysis welcoming, evidence-aware, and safe for people who have not seen every film.

## Contribution types
- **Observation:** A describable detail in a scene, such as composition, blocking, sound, editing, dialogue, or colour.
- **Interpretation:** A proposed meaning or reading of one or more observations.
- **Fan theory:** A more speculative explanation that may connect events or characters.
- **Production fact:** A factual claim about intent, production, or history that should have a credible source when possible.
- **Counter-reading:** A different interpretation of the same material.
- **Question:** A sincere request for clarification or alternative perspectives.

These categories are not a hierarchy of worth. Speculative readings are welcome when labelled honestly.

## Discussion norms
- Critique claims, not people.
- Explain why you disagree and cite a scene, observation, or source when possible.
- Do not present popularity as proof.
- Do not claim to know a filmmaker's private intent without credible evidence.
- Distinguish “the film shows” from “I interpret this as.”
- Credit sources and other contributors.
- No harassment, threats, hate, spam, or targeted abuse.
- No plagiarism or uncredited copying of essays and analyses.

## Spoilers
- Contributions must declare a spoiler level.
- Public previews should not reveal major spoilers.
- A user must deliberately reveal spoiler-gated content.
- Apply spoiler controls consistently across cards, search snippets, notifications, and metadata previews.
- Do not put spoilers in page titles or notification text.

## Moderation
Users can report abuse, plagiarism, spam, misinformation presented as fact, or copyright concerns.
Provide a moderation queue and basic actions: dismiss, hide, remove, warn, or suspend, with an audit trail as scope permits.

Do not automate punitive decisions solely from an AI model.
