# Personas and Research Plan

## Primary persona: The analytical cinephile
- Enjoys symbolism, visual composition, motifs, ambiguity, and narrative structure.
- Reads essays, watches video essays, writes long reviews, or participates in discussions.
- Frustration hypothesis: insightful interpretations and counterarguments become fragmented or difficult to revisit.

## Secondary persona: The film-club organiser
- Hosts screenings or discussion groups.
- Wants prompts, structured discussion, and a way to preserve useful analysis.
- Not the initial target unless interviews reveal a stronger need.

## Interview plan
Interview 8–12 cinephiles before committing to the full build.

### Ask about past behaviour
- Tell me about the last film you discussed in depth.
- Where did the discussion happen?
- Did you refer to a specific scene? How?
- Have you ever saved an interpretation or revisited a debate?
- What was frustrating or difficult?
- What tools did you try instead?
- Have you contributed to a film essay, forum, or fan theory? What motivated you?

Avoid leading questions like “Would you use an app that…?” Compliments and stated interest are weak evidence.

## Prototype test
Show a clickable prototype with:
1. A film page.
2. An interpretation with evidence.
3. A counter-reading.
4. A form to add an interpretation.

Observe whether users understand the difference between a claim and its evidence. Ask them to complete tasks without coaching.

## Validation signals
Positive:
- Users contribute an interpretation without being personally asked multiple times.
- Users read another interpretation and add a meaningful counter-reading.
- Users share a discussion with someone outside the test group.
- Users return to continue a discussion.

Weak signals:
- “This is cool.”
- Sign-ups without meaningful activity.
- Likes or votes without discussion.
- Activity that only occurs when the founder prompts users.

## Competitor research
Investigate products and adjacent categories including MovieStamp, FirstRoll, Kinolab, HardMatte, CineCode, long-form film essays, Reddit, and Letterboxd. Verify current features directly; do not assume the earlier research is exhaustive or current.

For each competitor, record:
- Target audience.
- Core user workflow.
- Evidence/scene linking.
- Alternative interpretations and rebuttals.
- Discovery and cross-film links.
- Moderation and spoiler controls.
- Pricing and licensing terms.
- What users praise or complain about.
- What specific job remains underserved.

Do not claim “first,” “only,” or “unavailable on the market” without strong evidence.
